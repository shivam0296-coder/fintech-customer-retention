01 / EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Why does FinTech matter for customer retention?

Banking is moving from traditional branch-centred services toward technology-enabled customer relationships. The research therefore examines whether FinTech can strengthen the relationship between customer satisfaction and customer retention.

01

FinTech

Financial technology that transforms how customers access payments, banking, financing and other financial services.

02

Satisfaction

The customer's evaluation of banking products, services and digital experiences.

03

Retention

The customer's continued relationship with the bank, including continued usage, preference and recommendation.

02 / FINTECH

Finance + Technology

FinTech refers to the use of technology to deliver, improve and innovate financial services.

In banking, this includes mobile banking, digital payments, online services, digital lending, data-driven services and technology-enabled customer support.

The practical objective is simple: make financial services faster, easier, more accessible and more convenient for customers.

03 / NEPAL CONTEXT

Nepal's banking ecosystem is becoming increasingly digital.

The research is positioned within Nepal's commercial banking sector, where digital financial services are becoming increasingly important to customer experience and banking strategy.

Regulatory Innovation

Digital-finance initiatives and technology-oriented financial innovation are becoming increasingly relevant to Nepal's financial ecosystem.

Digital Infrastructure

Mobile and internet-based channels allow banks to provide services beyond traditional physical branches.

Customer Expectations

Digital customers increasingly value speed, convenience, accessibility and security.

Strategic Challenge

Technology itself does not guarantee loyalty. The digital experience must create meaningful customer value.

04 / THEORETICAL FOUNDATION

Four perspectives explain the customer journey.

The thesis discusses technology adoption, trust, relationships and satisfaction to explain customer behaviour in digitally transforming banking.

Technology Acceptance Model

TAM emphasizes perceived usefulness and perceived ease of use. In digital banking, customers are more likely to adopt technology when it is useful and easy to operate.

Commitment–Trust Theory

Long-term relationships depend strongly on trust and commitment. Digital banking makes security, reliability and data protection particularly important.

Relationship Marketing

Relationship marketing focuses on long-term customer relationships, personalized service and sustained customer value rather than individual transactions.

Expectancy-Disconfirmation

Customer satisfaction is influenced by whether perceived service performance meets or exceeds expectations.

05 / CONCEPTUAL FRAMEWORK

The relationship being tested.

The central research idea is not simply whether FinTech influences customers. It asks whether FinTech changes the strength of the satisfaction–retention relationship.

09 / RESEARCH GAP

The missing piece.

Previous studies have examined satisfaction, trust, digital banking, service quality and retention. The thesis focuses on the more specific question of whether FinTech changes the satisfaction–retention relationship in Nepal.

Research Gap: FinTech's moderating role in Nepalese commercial banking.

The distinctive contribution of the proposed framework is to examine technology not only as an independent influence, but as a factor that may strengthen the conversion of customer satisfaction into customer retention.

10 / IMPLICATIONS

What can banks learn?

Design for Usefulness

Digital features should solve real customer problems rather than simply increase feature count.

Protect Trust

Security, privacy and reliable transaction execution are essential to digital banking relationships.

Reduce Friction

Faster payments, easier authentication and accessible digital services can improve customer experience.

Personalize Responsibly

Data-driven recommendations should remain transparent, relevant and customer-centric.

Measure Experience

Banks should connect digital adoption metrics with satisfaction and retention.

Think Beyond the App

Technology creates capability; sustainable retention requires a strong customer relationship.

“The strategic question is not whether a bank should adopt FinTech, but how effectively technology can transform customer experience into a durable banking relationship.”
11 / CONCLUSION

From digital convenience to long-term relationships.

The thesis frames FinTech as more than an operational tool. It is a potential relationship-strengthening mechanism.

When digital services are useful, easy, secure and responsive, they can contribute to positive customer experiences.

Those experiences can support satisfaction, while satisfaction can translate into continued relationships with the bank.
